
# BreaxCare – Breast Cancer Detection Application

BreaxCare is a mobile application designed to assist in the early detection of breast cancer through mammogram scanning, risk assessment, and medical history tracking. The app uses AI-driven analysis to provide quick and reliable risk level predictions while offering an interactive Q&A-based checkup to help users evaluate their symptoms

# Key Features
+ 📷 Mammogram Scanning & Risk Assessment
Users can upload their mammogram images for AI-based analysis.
The system provides a risk level (Low, Moderate, High) to help users understand their health status.

+ 🤖 Chat Bot (Q&A-Based Self-Assessment)
A built-in questionnaire helps users assess their risk based on symptoms and personal health history.
AI-powered responses provide recommendations on whether to seek medical attention.

+ 📊 Medical History Maintenance
Users can store and track past mammogram reports, risk levels, and medical consultations.
Provides insights into health trends over time.

+ 🏥 Secure and User-Friendly Experience
Simple & intuitive UI designed for ease of use.
Data encryption & security to protect sensitive medical information


# Technology Stack
+ Android Studio (Mobile App Development)
+ Python (Model & Image Processing)
+ SQLite (Database Management)
+ Java (Application Logic)

# How It Works
👉 Sign Up/Login – Create an account to track medical history.

👉 Upload Mammogram – Scans the image and displays the risk level.

👉 Quick Checkup – Answer a few health-related questions for an instant assessment.

👉 View Reports & Trends – Monitor past results and medical history.
